## Interpretation rules

- Owner evidence establishes current workflow facts; this register does not.
- Standards support control expectations, not proof that a control works locally.
- Vendor documentation proves capability, not outcome effectiveness.
- Implementation precedents support design comparisons, not benefit claims.
- Working papers, preprints, and framework claims retain their stated limitations.
