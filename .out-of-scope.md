# Out of scope

The first plugin slice does not:

- implement, merge, deploy, or operate software;
- fetch sources or assume connectors and filesystem access;
- decide truth, applicability, product intent, or acceptable risk deterministically;
- invoke the deferred `reviewable-delivery` workflow;
- create or promote RLP learnings;
- create, audit, or rewrite downstream Agent Skills from generated intent/specification;
- mutate a target repository;
- provide autonomous lifecycle orchestration;
- claim measured delivery improvement or ROI.

Those boundaries may change only through a separately reviewed specification and implementation slice.
