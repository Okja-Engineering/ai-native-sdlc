# Evidence schema

Every material claim record contains:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Claim | One falsifiable or clearly normative sentence. |
| Domain | Economics, intent, implementation, verification, governance, operations, learning, or measurement. |
| Source | Title, author/institution, date, and stable URL. |
| Evidence class | Standard/regulation, controlled empirical, observational empirical, vendor report, practitioner opinion, or hypothesis. |
| Population | Who, what systems, and how many observations were studied. |
| Outcome | What was actually measured; do not substitute activity for value. |
| Finding | Result with units and uncertainty when available. |
| Limitations | Design, sponsorship, generalizability, recency, and model/tool version. |
| Relevance | How the finding bears on sequential HITL delivery. |
| Verdict | Supported, qualified, contradicted, untested, or normative. |
| Confidence | High, medium, or low with one-sentence rationale. |

## Precedence

For outcome claims, prefer controlled empirical evidence, then strong observational evidence. Standards establish expected controls, not effectiveness. Vendor reports and thought-leader opinions identify practices and hypotheses but do not prove outcomes.

## Source independence

Trace repeated claims to their originating evidence. Multiple articles quoting one internal benchmark count as one source.
